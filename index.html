Free PNGTuber App [maruPNG]

A browser tool where your avatar moves in response to your mic audio

[ How to Use ]
  1. Prepare 3 images (16:9 recommended): Normal, Talking, and Blinking
  2. You can even use a single image for all three slots!
  3. Click the button to open the window and set each image
  4. Select the transparency color using the background button (Green, Blue, Magenta)
  5. Click "Start Mic" to sync your voice
  6. Click anywhere on the screen to hide the menu UI
